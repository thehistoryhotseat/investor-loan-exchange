Conforming (agency)

Conventional Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac loans, investment or owner-occupied, sized under the county conforming limit.

A note on conforming. Conforming loans on a primary or second home are consumer-purpose mortgages with their own rules; those scenarios are reviewed before any lender sees them. Investment-property conforming deals flow like every other lane.
For brokers

One form, once

The intake asks for the deal, not documents. It reaches every lender whose niche fits, and your scenario is shared only with the consent box checked.

Open the intake form
  • Contact details and role - borrower, broker, or agent
  • Loan purpose, product, amount, and target closing date
  • Property address, type, units, price or value, rehab budget, ARV
  • Borrower entity, credit band, experience, and liquidity
  • DSCR deals: rent, taxes, insurance, HOA
  • Conforming deals: occupancy and loan size vs. the county limit
  • Exit strategy and anything else worth knowing
For lenders

How lenders plug in

Each lender picks how its rates get answered - from a weekly email rhythm to a fully automatic agent.

Week one

Reply by email

Each fitting scenario lands with the lender's account executive as one structured rate request. The AE replies with pricing; every quote is logged.

Top lenders

Guidelines on file

The lender's niche rules and current rate sheets are kept on file, so fit and indicative pricing are answered automatically, straight from the current sheet.

Most automatic

The lender's own agent

The lender runs its own agent holding its guidelines and rates. It answers fit and pricing directly as scenarios arrive, under the lender's own sharing rules.

What a lender needs to join: its niche lanes (products, states, property types, loan size range), a current rate sheet or named pricing contact, one inbox for scenario requests, and a typical quote turnaround time. In return it sees only scenarios that fit, each as one structured request, with a log of everything it was sent and every quote it returned.
For AI agents

Agents are first-class submitters

Claude, ChatGPT, Meta, or a broker's own assistant can work with the exchange directly.

An agent submits the same way a person does: it reads the scenario format below, fills the intake form field for field, and gives a contact it can be reached through. Submitting by voice

A voice note reaches the exchange through an assistant that can hear it:

  1. The broker's own agent transcribes the voice note and fills the intake form from it. Works today, no setup on this side.
  2. The broker sends the voice note by WhatsApp to the exchange's assistant, which transcribes it and replies with the filled-in scenario to confirm before anything is submitted.

Ground rules for agents

The contact fields carry the broker's or borrower's details, not the agent's. Name the preparing agent under "anything else" in the form. Quotes and questions always come back to the person on the scenario - the agent prepares, the broker decides.

Same consent. Agent submissions are covered by the same consent checkbox: scenarios are shared with lenders only, no personal documents, and the broker can be contacted about the deal.
