Products

Lanes we route

Outside these lanes? Submit anyway; it is reviewed by hand.

DSCR rental

Long-term rental loans qualified on the property's cash flow. Priced on DSCR, LTV and credit.

Fix & flip

Short-term purchase and rehab funding, sized on budget and ARV. Priced on ARV, loan-to-cost and experience.

Bridge

Fast interim financing while a deal transitions. Priced on equity, the exit and speed.

Ground-up construction

Draw-based funding from lot to completion. Priced on budget, experience and the exit.

Cash-out refinance

Pull equity out of an existing investment property. Priced on LTV, credit and cash flow.

Conforming (agency)

Conventional Fannie / Freddie pricing, under the county limit. Priced on credit, LTV and DTI.

A note on conforming. The conforming lane covers investment properties only. Owner-occupied homes are consumer-purpose mortgages and aren't accepted in this draft.

        For AI agents
        
Readable by your agent

      

    

    
The site ships a machine-readable llms.txt and a JSON scenario schema. An agent can build a valid scenario from a conversation; the schema spells out every field. Paste the file into the loader above the intake and it prefills for your review.

    
    
For your agent: fetch /investor-loan-exchange/llms.txt and /investor-loan-exchange/scenario.schema.json, validate against the schema, and present the result for review. Never include SSNs, account numbers, or documents.
